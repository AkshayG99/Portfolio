- Built a real-time geospatial data pipeline and fine-tuned a **YOLOv11** model for early fire detection with high recall.
- Developed a full-stack dashboard for live inference, alerting, and heat-map visualization of fire-risk regions.
- Implemented a **Twilio notification system** to automatically text/call subscribed users and emergency dispatchers when a fire is detected.

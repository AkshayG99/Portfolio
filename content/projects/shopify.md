- Built **ShopFit** as a Shop Mini that uses AI to recommend outfits based on user data, Shopify products, and preferences.
- Utilized the **Shop Minis SDK** in order to collect user data, access product specifications, and fetch relevant products.
- Implemented **AI virtual try-on** (photo + video) and theme-based search layered on Shopify product catalogs.
- Received feedback and mentorship from a Shopify Engineering Manager and a Shopify Senior Product Operations Manager.
- Presented to **Farhan Thawar (Shopify Head of Engineering)** who commended the project's vision and execution.

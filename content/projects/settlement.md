- Placed **3rd globally** out of over 26,000 students worldwide, achieving international recognition for our innovative space settlement design and robotics logistics.
- Presented at the International Space Developement Conference (ISDC), Ontario legislative assembly, and Brampton City Hall. 
- Utilized high-fidelity **CAD (SolidWorks)** to develop full-scale 3D models and drone renderings, specifying mechanical systems, sensor integrations, and thruster placement.
- Developed an autonomous asteroid mining simulation in **Gazebo**, implementing ROS control scripts to test robotic drones executing path planning, obstacle avoidance, and drilling tasks on low-gravity surfaces.
- Performed **Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** to stress test the structural integrity of the toroidal space settlement's outer hull and support ribs against atmospheric pressure, centrifugal force, and thermal gradients.
